Description

Mahjong Sundae is a mahjong solitaire where the classic characters and bamboos are replaced by hand-drawn treats: cherries, donuts, sundaes, macarons and more. Each family of sweets has its own color and its own shape, so the tiles stay readable at a glance, even without the colors.

Tap two matching open tiles and the pair flies away. A tile is open when nothing lies on top of it and its left or right side is clear. Chain matches quickly to raise the combo, and clear the board to win the level. There is no timer: every level is about seeing what each stack hides.

Features

  • Large, easy-to-read tiles, designed for one-handed play in portrait.
  • Undo, Hint and Shuffle to think it through; every board is solvable, and a shuffle always leaves a solution.
  • Ten handcrafted levels, then endless generated boards, with Hard and Very hard levels announced in advance.
  • Each level won adds a scoop to a giant sundae; a daily challenge, the same for every player, and a daily coin gift.
  • Coins earned while playing unlock board and tile styles; they never change the gameplay.
  • Adjustable sound, vibration and visual effects; the game follows the Android "Remove animations" setting.
