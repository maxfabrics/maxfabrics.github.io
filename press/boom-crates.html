Description

Crates hang on chains under the rail of a crane. The cannon at the bottom of the screen fires upward as long as you keep your finger down: aim at a crate to break it, or cut its chain to drop everything it holds at once. A level is cleared when every crate has fallen or broken.

Explosive crates cut nearby chains and set off chain reactions, stone crates only fall when their chain snaps, and every tenth level is a Boss level. The game is played with one thumb, in portrait, in short sessions.

Features

  • Wood, glass, stone, ice, metal, gold, ammo and explosive crates.
  • Garlands, pallets, shelves, sliding trolleys, see-saws, pulleys, helium balloons and a conveyor belt.
  • Five handmade levels, then generated levels with no end; four factory zones and a Boss level every ten.
  • One to three stars per level, a daily challenge shared by every player, cosmetic cannons and cannonballs.
  • Adjustable sound, vibration and visual effects; the game follows the Android "Remove animations" setting.
