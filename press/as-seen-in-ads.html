Description

Everyone has seen those ads: an army of soldiers running down a bridge, number gates, bullets everywhere. Many players went looking for that game and found something else. As Seen In Ads is that game, made for real.

Slide one finger and the whole army follows. Take the right gates (+30, ×2) to multiply your soldiers, shoot the bad ones to flip them, break numbered barrels for better guns, crush the enemy squads, then dodge the red zones of the boss at the end of the bridge. After the boss, a bonus track from ×1 to ×10 pays for every soldier left standing. A level takes about forty seconds.

Features

  • A crowd of hundreds of soldiers within the first minutes, with honest numbers: what a gate shows is what you get.
  • Six bosses, each with its own attack to dodge, including a giant chest on bonus levels.
  • An arsenal of six guns, from the pistol to the rocket launcher, and upgrades that stay.
  • Five settings, a new one every five levels, endless levels and an infinite mode.
  • Daily missions, a daily challenge the same for every player, and a star chest.
  • No base to build, no alliance, no energy to wait for. Plays offline, made for one-handed play in portrait.
