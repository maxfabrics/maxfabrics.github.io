Description

Snugzle is a logic game set in a cozy home. Every pet has its quirks: the cat wants the sun, the parrot wants to be up high, the dog can't stand the cat. Each pet shows its wishes as icons, without a word of text: sun, warmth, height, a heart for the friend it wants to sleep next to, a broken heart for the one it never wants beside it.

Tap a pet, then its spot, or drag it there: the spots that don't suit it go dark. In the right spot it is delighted and falls asleep; in the wrong one it grumbles and costs one of three hearts. Every level has exactly one solution, and reasoning always finds it: no guessing needed.

Features

  • Small rooms with 4 pets to learn, then up to 9 pets on three floors, with Hard and Very hard levels announced in advance.
  • Endless generated levels, each one proven to have a single solution reachable by pure logic.
  • A hint that names the next pet to place and explains why, instead of just revealing the answer.
  • Nine hand-drawn pets and clue icons designed to stay readable even without colors.
  • A house that gets decorated with every level won, a daily challenge with a timer, the same for every player, and a daily coin gift.
  • Coins earned while playing unlock wallpapers and pet outfits; they never change the gameplay.
  • Adjustable sound, vibration and visual effects; designed for one-handed play in portrait.
