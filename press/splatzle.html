Description

Splatzle is a color nonogram: every grid hides a pixel picture. The numbers at the edge of each row and column give the length of each run of cells to paint, and their color tells you which color to use. Paint the right cells, and the picture appears, then comes alive.

Pick a color from the palette, then tap a cell or slide your finger along a line to paint several. A mistake shows the right cell and costs one of three hearts, and a finished line crosses out its empty cells by itself. Every grid has a single solution, and reasoning always finds it, step by step: no guessing needed.

Features

  • 5 × 5 grids to learn, then 8 × 8, 10 × 10 and 12 × 12, up to five colors, with Hard and Very hard grids announced in advance.
  • 60 pictures drawn for the game, then endless grids, each one checked by a solver.
  • A showcase of finished pictures, sorted into collections: Factory, Treats, Animals, Home, Nature. A complete collection earns a chest of coins.
  • A “Reveal a line” aid that paints at once the line with the most cells left.
  • A daily challenge, the same for every player, and a coin gift every day you play.
  • Coins earned by playing unlock grid themes; they never change the game.
  • Plays offline, no Wi-Fi needed; adjustable sound, vibration and visual effects; made for one-handed play in portrait.
