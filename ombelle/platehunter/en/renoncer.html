Withdraw from contract

PlateHunter Plus and “Remove ads” · Last updated: November 8, 2026

You can withdraw from a purchase made in PlateHunter within 14 days of concluding it, without giving any reason. Purchases go through Google Play.

Withdraw now

  1. For PlateHunter Plus, first stop the subscription in Google Play so that it does not renew.
  2. Then confirm your request: a ready-to-send email opens, with your @username, the purchase and its date to fill in.

Confirm my withdrawal My Google Play subscriptions

What happens next

  • We acknowledge receipt of your request by email, without delay.
  • PlateHunter Plus: the free trial is never charged. If you withdraw after the paid period has started, only the part of the service already provided remains due; we ask Google Play to refund the rest to your payment method.
  • “Remove ads”: before buying, you asked for immediate access and acknowledged losing your right of withdrawal once it is provided; that right therefore no longer applies once the purchase has been provided.
  • Withdrawing deletes nothing from your notebook.
