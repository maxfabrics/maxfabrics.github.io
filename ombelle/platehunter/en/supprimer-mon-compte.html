Delete your PlateHunter account

Last updated: November 8, 2026

PlateHunter is an app by Ombelle, a MaxFabrics brand (Maxence Bonnet, sole proprietorship, SIREN 104406707).

From the app (fastest)

  1. Open PlateHunter and tap your avatar at the top right: your profile opens.
  2. Tap Delete my account. The app first offers to save your data to a file.
  3. Confirm: your password, or your sign-in service (Google), asks you to prove it is really you.

Deletion is immediate.

Without the app

Write to ombelle@maxfabrics.com, subject “PlateHunter account deletion”, from your account’s email address (or your Google account’s), giving your @username. We delete the account and the data described below within 30 days at most, and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: identifier, email address, name, username and settings.
  • Your notebook: your restaurants, visits, ratings, comments and dish ratings, trash included.
  • Your links with your Hunters, your requests and your invitations.

What remains

  • Restaurants and photos shared during a meal stay with the other guests, under “Former member”.
  • Our servers’ nightly backup copies are cleared of it within 120 days at most.
  • Purchases stay in Google Play, under its own rules: stop any subscription in Google Play before deleting your account.
