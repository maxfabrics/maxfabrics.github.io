Your photos and codes stay on your phone

Copyglow privacy policy, the code scanner from Ombelle · Last updated: November 10, 2026

Copyglow reads a code from a photo (gift card, Wi-Fi key, license key, promo code, serial number, IBAN) and copies it to the clipboard. Reading happens on your phone: the photo and the code are never sent to a server, not even ours, because we don't have one. There is no account to create. The free version shows an ad banner, which works as explained below.

Who is responsible

The app is published by Maxence Bonnet, sole proprietor (MaxFabrics, SIREN 104406707), under the Ombelle brand. For any question about your data: ombelle@maxfabrics.com.

Camera and images

The app only accesses the images you give it:

  • the camera, only when you take a photo from the app's viewfinder;
  • an image from your gallery, through Android's photo picker: the app only sees the image you choose, never the rest of your gallery;
  • an image you share from another app.

Reading the code

The photo is read on your phone by ML Kit, a Google component built into the app, using a model installed on the phone. The app keeps a working copy in its private storage, deleted as soon as you return to the viewfinder or read another image. The ML Kit component sends Google technical data about its own operation (device model, app version, installation identifier, performance measurements), never your photos or codes.

The clipboard

The code found is copied to the Android clipboard and marked as sensitive: Android hides it in its preview. As with anything copied, other apps may be able to read it under Android's rules; paste it where you need it, then copy something else if you want to erase it.

History

If you keep it turned on, the app keeps your last 50 copied codes on this phone, encrypted by Android's secure storage (key kept in the Keystore). They are shown masked. Nothing is copied to Android's automatic backup or to transfers to a new phone. You can delete a code, erase everything, or turn off history in the settings: it is then erased and nothing more is kept.

Advertising

The free version shows a Google AdMob banner, at the bottom of the result and of the history. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google form first asks for your consent; you can review your choices at any time in Settings › Privacy choices. To display and measure ads, Google may receive:

  • your phone's advertising identifier (which you can reset or delete in Android's settings);
  • your IP address and device information (model, language, Android version);
  • your interactions with the ads (impressions, taps).

What advertising never receives

Ads are only personalized if you accept. The app never sends your photos, your codes or your history to advertising. Google processes this data under its own rules: policies.google.com/privacy.

Removing ads

The “Remove ads” purchase goes through Google Play, which processes payment under its own rules (policies.google.com/privacy). The app never sees your payment method; it only asks Google Play whether the purchase was made, and keeps that on the phone. Once ads are removed, no advertising component is started anymore. As with any seller, Google Play gives us sales reports (order number, product, amount, country), without your name or payment method; we keep them for the time required by our accounting obligations.

Google Play statistics

If you agreed to share usage and diagnostics data with Google on your phone, Google Play may show us anonymous, aggregated crash statistics. The app contains no audience measurement tool.

If you write to us

Your message and your address are used only to reply to you. Do not include your codes.

Age

The app is intended for people aged 18 and over.

Legal basis

The app's operation that you request (reading and copying a code, keeping history); your consent for personalized advertising and advertising trackers; the contract for the purchase; our legal obligations for sales reports.

How long

A photo is kept only for as long as it takes to read it. History stays on your phone until you erase it or uninstall the app.

Erasing your data

In the app: delete a code from history, or Settings › Keep history, turned off, to erase everything. Uninstalling the app erases everything it keeps on the phone. For your other rights, write to ombelle@maxfabrics.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr).

If this policy changes

The date at the top of this page changes with it. Any new use of your data would first require your consent.
