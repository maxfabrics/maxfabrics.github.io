Withdraw from the contract

BookHunter Plus and “Remove ads” · Last updated: 12 November 2026

You can withdraw from a purchase made in BookHunter within 14 days of its conclusion, without giving a reason. Purchases go through Google Play.

Withdraw now

  1. For BookHunter Plus monthly or yearly, first stop the subscription in Google Play, so that it does not renew.
  2. Then confirm your request: a ready-to-send email opens, with your name, your @username, the purchase and its date to fill in.

Confirm withdrawal My Google Play subscriptions

What happens next

  • We send you without delay, by email, an acknowledgement of receipt that repeats your request, with its date and time.
  • BookHunter Plus monthly or yearly: the free trial is never charged to you. If you withdraw after the paid period has started, only the share of the service already provided remains due; we ask Google Play to refund the rest, which it pays back to your payment method.
  • Plus lifetime and “Remove ads”: before the purchase, you asked for immediate access and acknowledged losing your right of withdrawal once it is provided; this right therefore no longer applies once the purchase has been provided.
  • Withdrawing erases nothing from your library.
