BookHunter terms of use · Version of 12 November 2026

What the app is for

BookHunter is for keeping your library and your reading log, and for sharing them with the friends you accept: lendable books, loans, exchanges, gifts and shared reads. BookHunter is published by Maxence Bonnet, sole trader (SIREN 104406707), under the Ombelle brand. Book records come from public sources (Bibliothèque nationale de France, Open Library) or from what you enter.

Your account

You must be at least 18: your date of birth is asked once when you sign up, and we keep only the result, never the date. You sign in with Google or with an email address of your own, which you confirm. Keep your access to yourself, and let us know if someone else uses your account. Your username must neither pass you off as someone else nor be insulting, hateful or obscene. Your username, your name and your photo are visible to any signed-in person who knows your username: a first name is enough.

What you publish

Your notes, reviews, quotes and photos remain yours. You allow us to keep them and to show them to the people you share them with (your friends, the participants in a shared read), for as long as the service requires. What you have shared with others (a loan, an exchange, a note in a shared read) may stay with them if you delete your account, under “Former member”.

Loans, exchanges and gifts

BookHunter helps you keep track of the books that circulate between friends. Loans, exchanges and gifts are settled between you: BookHunter guarantees neither their return nor their condition, and does not take part in your arrangements. No payment goes through the app for a loan, an exchange or a gift; a book “for sale” is only a note for yourself.

What is forbidden

Anything illegal, and in particular: hatred and discrimination, harassment, insults and threats, nudity and sexual content, violence, any content that sexualises or endangers a child, impersonation, fraud and scams, unsolicited advertising, infringement of the rights of others (including copying protected works beyond a short quotation), and photos of people without their consent.

Reporting and blocking

You can block a person and report a person or a piece of content from the app. Anyone, even without an account, can report illegal content to us at ombelle@maxfabrics.com: say where it is and why it seems illegal to you, give your name and your email address, and state your good-faith belief that your report is accurate and complete.

What we decide

We acknowledge receipt of every report, examine it carefully and impartially, and tell its author what we decide and how to challenge it. Reports are sorted by seriousness according to the reason chosen; a person examines them and decides. We may remove content, or limit or suspend an account, temporarily or permanently, in proportion to the seriousness and repetition, including for repeated false reports. The person concerned receives a statement of reasons (the measure and its duration, the facts, the rule of these terms or the law concerned) and how to appeal, and we do not say who made the report unless it is essential. You can challenge a decision by writing to us, or before the courts. We alert the authorities when the law requires it, notably in the face of a threat to someone's life or safety. These decisions reach you in the app.

Purchases

The free version shows ads, labelled as such. Purchases and subscriptions go through Google Play, which bills and refunds them under its own rules, and they apply to your account. BookHunter Plus monthly or yearly renews automatically until you cancel it in Google Play; a free trial becomes paid when it ends, unless cancelled before. Plus lifetime and “Remove ads” are paid once. Stopping a subscription erases nothing from your library. To withdraw from the contract, use “Withdraw from contract here” on the Plus screen. We do our best to keep the service running without interruption. You keep the guarantees the law gives you, including the legal guarantee of conformity. French law applies, without removing any right that the law of your country gives you.
