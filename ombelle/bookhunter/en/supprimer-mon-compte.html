Delete your BookHunter account

Last updated: 12 November 2026

BookHunter is an app by Ombelle, a MaxFabrics brand (Maxence Bonnet, sole trader, SIREN 104406707).

From the app (the fastest way)

  1. Open BookHunter, then Settings.
  2. Tap Delete my account. Remember to export your books first if you want to keep them (Settings › Backup).
  3. Confirm: your password, or your Google account, asks you to prove it is really you.

Deletion is immediate.

Without the app

Write to ombelle@maxfabrics.com, subject “BookHunter account deletion”, from your account's email address (or your Google account's), giving your @username. We delete the account and the data described below within 30 days at most, and reply to you once it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: identifier, email address, name, username, photo and settings.
  • The online copy of your library and your photos, on our relay.
  • Your friendships, your blocks, your pending requests and invitations.
  • The library on the phone where you delete your account, bin included.

What remains

  • What you have shared with others (a loan, an exchange, a note in a shared read) may stay with them, under “Former member”.
  • Reports and moderation decisions, for as long as needed to meet our legal obligations.
  • Purchases remain in Google Play, under its own rules: stop any subscription in Google Play before deleting your account.
  • A backup you have exported stays wherever you put it.
