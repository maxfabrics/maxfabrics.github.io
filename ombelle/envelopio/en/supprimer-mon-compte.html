Delete your Envelopio account

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Envelopio is an app by Ombelle, a MaxFabrics brand (Maxence Bonnet, sole proprietor, SIREN 104406707). The account is optional: without an account, nothing is kept anywhere other than on your phone.

From the app (fastest)

Deletion is immediate:

  • open Envelopio, then Settings, and tap your account card;
  • tap “Delete my account”;
  • confirm: your password, or your Google account, asks you to prove it is really you.

Without the app

Write to ombelle@maxfabrics.com, subject “Envelopio account deletion”, from your account’s email address (or your Google account’s). We delete the account and the data described below within 30 days at most, and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

In our cloud (Google Firebase):

  • your account: ID, email address, first name or nickname, color;
  • the online copy of your budgets;
  • your place in budgets for two and your pending invitations.

What remains

With no link to any account:

  • your budgets on your phone, until you erase them (Settings › Erase everything);
  • in a budget for two, the other person’s copy, with what you recorded together in it;
  • a backup you exported, wherever you put it;
  • your purchase of the plus version, with Google Play, under its own rules.
