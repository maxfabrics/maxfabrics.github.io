Your budget stays on your phone

Privacy policy of Envelopio, the Ombelle envelope budgeting app · Last updated: 10 November 2026

Envelopio lets you keep your budget with the envelope method. Your amounts, envelopes and notes stay on your phone: we have no server that receives them, there is no account to create and no access to your bank. The free version shows an advertising banner from Google AdMob: it receives data from your device, never your budget. The plus version shows no advertising.

Who is responsible

The app is published by Maxence Bonnet, sole proprietor (MaxFabrics, SIREN 104406707), under the Ombelle brand. For any question about your data: ombelle@maxfabrics.com.

What you enter

Everything you enter is kept on this phone only:

  • your budgets: income, envelopes, expenses and their dates, notes, savings goals and challenges;
  • your settings: country and currency, payday, reminders, app color;
  • the fact that you bought the plus version.

How it is protected

The budget is written to an encrypted file (AES-256) in the app's private folder, with a backup copy that is encrypted too. The encryption key is kept in Android's secure storage (Keystore), not in the file. Google's automatic backups and the transfer to a new phone are switched off for this app: your budget does not leave the phone that way. One exception: if you place the balances widget (plus version) on the home screen, the app keeps what it needs to display it (the month, “To assign”, the first four envelopes with their amounts) in its private preferences, without the budget's encryption. Those lines appear only on the home screen, never on the lock screen; “Erase everything” removes them, and the app removes them the next time it opens once the widget is removed; until then, they stay in its private preferences.

What can leave the phone

Only what you start yourself, to the place you choose (Downloads, Drive, a messaging app…):

  • an encrypted backup: an .envx file protected by a password you choose (at least 8 characters). This password is stored nowhere: if you forget it, we cannot reopen the backup;
  • the month summary image, with or without the amounts, which you share through Android's share sheet;
  • with the plus version, a CSV or PDF export of your budget, which is not encrypted.

After a share

We have no access to these files. Once they are out, their protection depends on where you keep them and on the service that receives them, which has its own policy. During a share, the app leaves a temporary copy in its private cache: it is removed at the next launch once it is more than an hour old, and by “Erase everything”.

Advertising in the free version

The free version shows a Google AdMob banner at the bottom of the main screen. It receives nothing from your budget: the ad request contains no amount, no envelope name and no note. For advertising, measurement and fraud prevention, Google's SDK does receive the following from your device, according to Google's documentation and under its policy (policies.google.com/privacy):

  • your IP address, and therefore an approximate location;
  • your interactions with ads;
  • technical information about how the SDK runs (diagnostics);
  • device identifiers, including the advertising ID depending on your choices.

Your choices about advertising

In Europe, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, Google first shows a consent form: you can refuse personalization, and the app then requests only non-personalized ads. The advertising SDK starts only after the answer to this form, which you can reopen at any time in Settings › Ad choices. Elsewhere, no form is shown and ads may be personalized, unless you have turned personalization off in your phone's Android settings, where you can also reset or delete your advertising ID. Whatever you choose, the app works the same way.

Plus version: no advertising

With the plus version, the app shows no advertising, does not show the consent form and does not start the advertising SDK. One exception: on a new phone or after a reinstall, the app waits for Google Play's answer about your purchase, ten seconds at most, before launching advertising. If Google Play does not answer in time, the free version may start advertising, and show the consent form where it is required, before your purchase is found; the banner disappears as soon as Google Play returns it.

Buying the plus version

The one-time purchase, with no subscription, goes through Google Play, which handles the payment under its own rules (policies.google.com/privacy). The app never sees your payment method. It asks Google Play for the product's price and whether you have already bought it (at each launch), and keeps on the phone only the fact that the plus version is active, to unlock it even offline. The Google Play component built into the app for purchases also sends Google technical data about its own operation, never your budget. As with any seller, Google Play gives us sales records (order number, product, amount, country), without your name or payment method; we keep them for as long as our accounting obligations require.

Reminders

The payday (9 am) and end-of-month (6 pm) reminders are off until you turn them on. They are notifications scheduled on your phone, with no server, and they contain no amount or envelope name. On Android 13 and later, Android asks your permission to show notifications the first time you turn one on. “Erase everything” removes them.

Android permissions

The app does not ask for your location, contacts, photos or microphone. It notably declares access to the Internet (banner, consent form, Google Play), the advertising ID (which Google's SDK reads depending on your choices, in the free version), notifications (only if you turn on a reminder) and the phone's startup (to reschedule those reminders). It also declares the network state and vibration (reminders, touch feedback). Built-in components (the advertising SDK, the widgets) add keeping the phone awake and a background service, and the advertising SDK adds access to Android's advertising information (ID, attribution, interests: Android's Privacy Sandbox). These advertising accesses are used only in the free version and according to your choices. Backup and export files go through Android's document picker and share sheet, with no storage permission.

No account, no audience measurement

There is no account to create: the app asks for neither your name nor your email address. It has no audience measurement tool, no crash reporting tool and no server of ours that receives data.

Google Play statistics

If you have agreed to share usage and diagnostics data with Google on your phone, Google Play may show us crash statistics, anonymous and aggregated.

If you write to us

Your message and your address are used only to reply to you. Please don't attach your budget or your amounts.

Age

The app is for people aged 18 and over and does not target minors.

Legal basis

Your consent (GDPR, article 6) for the free version's advertising, given in Google's form where it is required; the contract for the purchase; our legal obligations for sales records. We do not process your budget: it stays on your phone. You can withdraw your consent at any time: where Google requires the form (Europe, the United Kingdom, Switzerland), in Settings › Ad choices; elsewhere, in your phone's Android settings (where you can also reset your advertising ID); or by switching to the plus version.

How long

Your budget stays on your phone until you erase it: the app sends it nowhere and does not erase it by itself. The data received through advertising is kept by Google under its own policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

Erasing your data

Settings › Erase everything, then confirm (the app first offers to make a backup): the budget, its backup copy, its encryption key, your reminders, your color, the widget balances and the share copies are deleted, with no way back. Uninstalling the app also deletes its data. Files you saved or shared yourself (backup, export, summary) stay where you put them: delete them there. Your purchase is with Google Play: “Erase everything” does not touch it.

Your rights

We do not hold your budget: you view, correct and export it in the app. The encrypted backup keeps a complete copy, and, with the plus version, the CSV export opens in a spreadsheet and the PDF export in a PDF reader. For your rights over the few data we do hold (sales records, messages you write to us) or for any other question (access, rectification, erasure, objection, restriction, portability), write to ombelle@maxfabrics.com. You can also complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French data protection authority, or to your own.

What the app does not do

The app connects to no bank, moves no money and gives no financial advice: it helps you keep track of your budget.

If this policy changes

The date at the top of this page changes with it. Any new use of your data would be announced here and, where the law requires it, subject to your agreement.
