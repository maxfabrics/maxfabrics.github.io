Envelopio account terms

Version 1 · 23 November 2026

Envelopio works without an account. The account is optional and free: it backs up your budget, finds it again on another phone and, with the plus version, shares it with a person you invite. Envelopio is published by Maxence Bonnet, sole proprietorship (MaxFabrics, SIREN 104406707), under the Ombelle brand.

Your account

You must be at least 18. You sign in with Google or with an email address of your own, which you confirm. Keep your access to yourself and let us know if someone else uses your account. The first name or nickname you choose must not make you pass for someone else, nor be insulting.

Backup and sync

With an account, a copy of your budget is kept online and your phones update each other. We do our best to keep it running without interruption and without loss, but an outage remains possible: the app’s encrypted backup (Settings › Backup) remains the best way to keep a copy of your own.

Budget for two

With the plus version, the owner of a budget can invite one person to it; the invited person joins it for free. You both see and edit this whole budget, and each of you sees the other’s first name and color. Only invite someone you want to share this information with. Either of you can leave the budget at any time, and the owner can remove the other person: they keep a copy of the budget on their phone, without the changes that follow. What you record together is settled between the two of you.

What is forbidden

Any unlawful use, and in particular: using someone else’s account, trying to read or edit budgets that are not yours, getting around the app’s limits, or using it to send emails or invitations in bulk.

What Envelopio is not

Envelopio connects to no bank, moves no money and gives no financial advice: it helps you keep track of your budget. The amounts are the ones you enter.

Purchases

The plus version is bought once in Google Play, which bills it and refunds it under its own rules; it applies on your phones connected to the same Google Play account. In case of a dispute, write to us first; you can then refer it free of charge to the consumer mediator: Société Médiation Professionnelle, www.mediateur-consommation-smp.fr, Alteritae, 5 rue Salvaing, 12000 Rodez, France.

Suspension and end

You can delete your account at any time from the app (Settings › your account › Delete my account) or by writing to us: your budgets then stay on your phone, without an account. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms, in a proportionate way, telling you why and how to challenge it. If we stopped the account service, we would tell you in the app at least 30 days beforehand, and your budgets would stay on your phone.

Your data

What the account keeps, where and for how long: see Envelopio’s privacy policy (maxfabrics.com/ombelle/envelopio/en/confidentialite).

Changes

If these terms change significantly, the app tells you and asks you to accept them before you keep using your account. You keep the guarantees that the law gives you, including the legal guarantee of conformity. French law applies, without removing any right that the law of your country gives you.
