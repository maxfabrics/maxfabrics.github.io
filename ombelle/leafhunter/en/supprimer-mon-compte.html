Delete your LeafHunter account

Last updated: 18 November 2026

LeafHunter is an app by Ombelle, a MaxFabrics brand (Maxence Bonnet, sole trader, SIREN 104406707).

From the app (the fastest way)

  1. Open LeafHunter, then your Profile.
  2. Tap Delete my account.
  3. Confirm: your password, or your Google account, asks you to prove it's really you.

Deletion is immediate.

Without the app

Write to ombelle@maxfabrics.com, subject “LeafHunter account deletion”, from your account's email address (or your Google account's), giving your @username. We delete the account and the data described below within 30 days at most, and reply to you once it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account: identifier, email address, name, username and settings.
  • The online copy of your jungle and your photos, on our relay, and the counters linked to your account.
  • Your friendships, your blocks, your pending requests and invitations.
  • The plants you showed, your posts and their comments, your votes, your plant-sitting and your cuttings in progress.
  • The jungle on the phone where you delete your account, and its photos.

What remains

  • What you have given to others (a cutting that became their plant, care given during a plant-sitting) stays with them.
  • Reports and moderation decisions, for as long as needed to meet our legal obligations.
  • Purchases remain in Google Play, under its own rules: stop any subscription in Google Play before deleting your account.
