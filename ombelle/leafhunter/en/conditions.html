LeafHunter terms of use · Version of 18 November 2026

What the app is for

LeafHunter is for keeping track of your plants (photos, care, reminders, light) and sharing them with the friends you accept: plants you show, feed, plant-sitting and cuttings. LeafHunter is published by Maxence Bonnet, sole trader (SIREN 104406707), under the Ombelle brand. Care advice is indicative: each plant and each room is different. Species recognition from a photo, by Pl@ntNet, can be wrong; the toxicity of a plant is given with its sources, but if in doubt for a child or an animal, ask a professional.

Your account

You must be at least 18 to create an account; before that, the app works on your phone, without an account or friends. You sign in with Google or with an email address of your own, which you confirm. Keep your access to yourself, and let us know if someone else uses your account. Your username must neither pass you off as someone else nor be insulting, hateful or obscene. Your username and your name are visible to any signed-in person who knows your username: a first name is enough.

What you publish

Your photos, posts and comments remain yours. You allow us to keep them and to show them to the people you share them with (your accepted friends), for as long as the service requires. What you have shared with others (a cutting given, care given during a plant-sitting) may stay with them if you delete your account.

Plant-sitting and cuttings

LeafHunter helps you entrust your plants to a friend and give away cuttings. These exchanges are settled between you: LeafHunter guarantees neither the condition of a plant that is looked after nor the success of a cutting, and does not take part in your arrangements. No payment goes through the app for a plant-sitting or a cutting. Invasive species banned in the European Union may not be given away.

What is forbidden

Anything illegal, and in particular: hatred and discrimination, harassment, insults and threats, nudity and sexual content, violence, any content that sexualises or endangers a child, impersonation, fraud and scams, unsolicited advertising, selling plants or products, infringement of the rights of others, and photos of people without their consent.

Reporting and blocking

You can block a person and report a person, a photo or a comment from the app. Anyone, even without an account, can report illegal content to us at ombelle@maxfabrics.com: say where it is and why it seems illegal to you, give your name and your email address, and state your good-faith belief that your report is accurate and complete.
