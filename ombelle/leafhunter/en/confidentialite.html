Your jungle stays yours

LeafHunter privacy policy · Last updated: 20 November 2026

LeafHunter keeps your jungle on your phone. Only what is needed to give it back to you on another device (an encrypted copy), what you show to your friends and the photo you have identified go online. Your plants, your photos and your notes are never sold and never used for advertising. The free version shows ads from Google AdMob, which receive device data (advertising ID, IP address), never your plants, your photos or your notes.

Who is responsible

LeafHunter is published by Maxence Bonnet, sole trader (MaxFabrics, SIREN 104406707), under the Ombelle brand. For any question about your data: ombelle@maxfabrics.com.

Why, and on what basis

Your account, your jungle, its online copy, your photos, your friends, what you share with them, your plant-sitting and your cuttings are used to run the service you asked for (performance of the contract). Purchases also rely on the contract, and keeping their records on our accounting obligations. Recognising a plant from a photo and personalised ads rely on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time; non-personalised ads, on our legitimate interest in funding the free version. Service security, moderation and the fight against abuse rely on our legitimate interest in protecting the service and its members, and on our legal obligations.

What you record

Your plants and their profiles (nickname, species, pot, potting mix, origin, notes), your rooms and their light measurements, your care, your reminders and your photos. All of this is saved on your phone first. None of it goes to the ad network, and the app does no audience measurement.

Where your data lives

Your jungle lives on your phone. So you can find it again on another device, a copy is sent to the LeafHunter relay (Cloudflare), which encrypts it with a key unique to your account before storing it in Cloudflare R2, in the European Union; your photos are stored the same way, encrypted. What you share with your friends (your profile, the plants you show them, your posts, reactions and comments, your votes, your plant-sitting and your cuttings) is stored with Google Firebase, in Paris; the photos you show them are stored, encrypted, on the relay. To block fraudulent access, Google (App Check and Play Integrity) checks that requests really come from the LeafHunter app.

What your friends see

Your profile (username and name) is visible to anyone who searches for your exact username, unless you have blocked them; your email address is never shown. Each plant stays private until you make it “visible to my friends”: they then see its nickname, species, cover photo and arrival date, never your notes. Your posts, reactions and comments are visible only to your accepted friends. When you entrust plants to a friend for a plant-sitting, they receive a copy of those plants and their care records (without photos or notes), and your instructions, for the length of the plant-sitting. In “My green corner”, your profile photo, a few words about you, your favourite plants (only those you already show them) and your room’s decor are visible to your accepted friends only; you can change or remove them at any time, and they are deleted with your account.

Recognising a plant

Only if you agree (you are asked before the first time): when you ask to recognise a plant's species, the photo goes through the LeafHunter relay to Pl@ntNet (CIRAD, Inria, INRAE and IRD, in Montpellier), which suggests species. Neither the relay nor Pl@ntNet keeps the photo: it is only held in memory for as long as the recognition takes. No location and no name goes with the photo. To enforce the daily quota, the relay counts recognitions per account, or, before an account exists, by a fingerprint of the installation identifier, without the photo. To withdraw your consent, simply stop asking for recognitions.

Advertising

LeafHunter is free thanks to advertising: from time to time, an ad in your lists or at the very bottom of a page, marked “Ad”, never while you take a photo, log care, plant-sit, cut, or use the “My plant is unwell” questionnaire; and, only if you choose it, a video that gives you one extra recognition. Ads come from Google AdMob. Before any ad, the app asks for your choice through Google's consent form; with your agreement, Google receives technical information about your phone (advertising ID, IP address, model, language, interactions with ads) to show ads, limit how often they repeat, measure them and, if you accept, personalise them. If you refuse, ads are still shown, but non-personalised, or “limited” if you also refuse storage on your phone; Google processes this information under its own rules (policies.google.com/technologies/ads). Your plants, your photos and your notes are never sent to it. To credit a video, the relay keeps the reference Google sends it. In the European Union, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, you can change your mind at any time: Profile › Ad choices. With “Remove ads”, no ad is shown: only the video is still offered, if you choose it. With LeafHunter Plus, no advertising at all.

Purchases

Purchases (“Remove ads” and LeafHunter Plus, monthly, yearly or lifetime) go through Google Play, which processes the payment: LeafHunter never sees your card or your payment method. To grant you the purchase, the app sends the relay the purchase token issued by Google Play; the relay checks it with Google, then keeps a fingerprint (SHA-256) of that token; for a subscription, it also keeps the token itself, needed to check each renewal. It also keeps the product purchased, its expiry date and your account identifier, without your name or your email address, for as long as the purchase is valid. A refunded purchase stops being granted.

Your account

Your account is created after your first plant, with Google, or with an email address and a password that you confirm. Your email address stays with Google Firebase's sign-in service and is never shown to other people. You are asked whether you are 18: we keep only the answer, never a date. Under 18, the app works without an account: your jungle stays on your phone, with no friends and no online copy.

What is required

To create an account, you need an email address (or a Google account), a username and to confirm that you are 18: without them, no account, and the app stays on your phone. Everything else (photos, plant recognition, friends, plant-sitting) is optional. No decision that has legal effects for you or significantly affects you is made solely by automated means: the age check and the daily quotas apply fixed rules, which you can challenge by writing to us.

Phone permissions

The camera is used to photograph your plants; the light sensor, to measure the light in a room when you ask for it; notifications, for the daily reminder, scheduled on your phone. LeafHunter uses neither your location nor your contacts: you choose your hemisphere by hand.

Who receives your data

Only the providers that run the service, each for its role described here: Google (Firebase, App Check, AdMob, Google Play), Cloudflare (relay, encrypted copy, photos) and Pl@ntNet (plant recognition, in France). Google and Cloudflare may process data outside the European Union, notably in the United States: these transfers rely on the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or, for certified companies, on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework; a copy of these safeguards is sent to you on request at ombelle@maxfabrics.com. We never sell your data.

Reports and moderation

When you report a person or a piece of content, we receive your report, its reason and the content concerned; the reported person does not know who reported them. Reports and decisions are kept for as long as needed to handle them and to meet our legal obligations.

Sharing and backups

What you choose to send (invitation, timelapse) goes through the app you choose. If Android backup is turned on on your phone, Google may copy the app's data to it, encrypted by Android, to give it back to you on a new phone.

How long

Your data stays as long as your account exists. The feed shows your friends' 50 most recent posts. When you delete your account, its online copy and your photos are erased from the relay immediately; your account, your profile, your username, your friends, the plants you showed, your posts and their comments, your plant-sitting, your cuttings and your pending requests are erased; the relay's counters linked to your account too, and the jungle on this phone is emptied. A request made by email is handled within 30 days at most.

Correcting or erasing your data

You can edit or delete each item from the app. To delete your account: Profile › Delete my account. Without the app: maxfabrics.com/ombelle/leafhunter/en/supprimer-mon-compte, or an email to ombelle@maxfabrics.com. You also have the right to access your data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict its use, to object to it, to withdraw your consent and to get it back in a readable format (portability): write to ombelle@maxfabrics.com. You can also lodge a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr), the French data protection authority, or with the data protection authority of your country.
