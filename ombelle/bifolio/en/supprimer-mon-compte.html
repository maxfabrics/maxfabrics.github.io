Delete your Bifolio account

Last updated: 25 November 2026

Bifolio is an app by Ombelle, a MaxFabrics brand (Maxence Bonnet, sole proprietor, SIREN 104406707). The account is optional: without an account, nothing is kept anywhere but on your phone. Contact: ombelle@maxfabrics.com.

From the app (fastest)

Deletion is immediate:

  • open Bifolio, then Settings, and tap your account card;
  • tap “Delete my account”;
  • confirm: your password, or your Google account, asks you to prove it is really you.

Without the app

Write to ombelle@maxfabrics.com, subject “Bifolio account deletion”, from your account’s email address (or your Google account’s). We delete the account and the data described below within 30 days at most, and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

In our cloud (Google Firebase):

  • your account: identifier, email address, first name, color;
  • the online copy of your library: titles, progress, reading direction;
  • your reading settings and statistics.

What remains

With no link to any account:

  • your library on your phone, until you erase it;
  • your files, in your folders: Bifolio never copied them;
  • an export you stored wherever you like;
  • your Bifolio Pro purchase, at Google Play, under its own rules.
