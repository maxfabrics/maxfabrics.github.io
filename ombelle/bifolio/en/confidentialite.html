Your books stay on your phone

Privacy policy of Bifolio, Ombelle's two-page reader · Last updated: 25 November 2026

Bifolio opens the comics, manga and e-books you own (CBZ, ZIP, image folders, PDF, EPUB) and shows them as two-page spreads. Your files stay on your phone: they never go through our services. An account is optional: without it, your library and progress also stay on your phone; with it, their copy follows your account so you find them on your other devices. The free version shows an ad banner in the library and settings, never while you read, which works as explained below.

Who is responsible

The app is published by Maxence Bonnet, sole trader (MaxFabrics, SIREN 104406707), under the Ombelle brand. For any question about your data: ombelle@maxfabrics.com.

Your files

The app only accesses the files and folders you give it, through Android's document picker. It asks for no permission over the whole phone storage.

  • it keeps permission to read the chosen folders and files again, to find your books at the next launch; you can remove a book from the library at any time, and the file is never modified or deleted;
  • pages are read and displayed on the phone: no file, title or page is ever sent anywhere;
  • a file opened from another app ("Open with Bifolio") is handled the same way.

Library, progress and settings

The app keeps on the phone the list of your books (title, location, file fingerprint), the page you are on, your settings, cover thumbnails and, if you keep them on, your reading statistics. The library, progress, settings and statistics follow Android backup (which you can turn off in Android settings) so they can be found again on a new phone; thumbnails and downloaded classics are not included. You can also export a copy to a file you keep wherever you like.

Free classics

The Free Library list is read from our website (maxfabrics.com); when you download a book, the file comes directly from Project Gutenberg, and a Pepper&Carrot episode directly from the Pepper&Carrot website. If you add the Standard Ebooks catalog, the app reaches it with your membership address, like a personal server. These sites see your phone's IP address, as with any web page; nothing else is sent to them.

Optional account and sync

If you create an account (with Google or an email address you confirm), we keep in Google Firebase (Cloud Firestore and Authentication, database servers in Europe): your email address, an identifier, the first name and color you choose, the version of the terms you accepted, and for each book its title, author, series, format, the fingerprint of its file (not the file), the page you are on, “finished” and the reading direction, along with your reading settings and statistics. This data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Google, and only you and the app access it through your account; it is not end-to-end encrypted. It is used only to give your library back on your other devices: never for advertising. Account emails (address confirmation, forgotten password) are sent by Firebase; for some French mailboxes, they may be sent by our email provider Brevo, which then only receives your address and the link.

Dictionary

When you long-press a word in a book, that word alone is sent to Wiktionary (Wikimedia) to show its definition; Wikimedia then sees your phone's IP address, as with any web page. Nothing else is sent and nothing is kept.

Your own server (Bifolio Pro)

If you add your Komga, Kavita or OPDS server, the app only contacts the address you entered. Your password or API key is encrypted by Android's secure storage and only sent to that server. This exchange happens between your phone and your server: we have no access to it.

Advertising

The free version shows a Google AdMob banner at the bottom of the library and settings. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, a Google form first asks for your consent; you can review your choices at any time in Settings › Privacy choices. To show and measure ads, Google may receive:

  • your phone's advertising ID (which you can reset or delete in Android settings);
  • your IP address and device information (model, language, Android version);
  • your interactions with ads (display, tap).

What advertising never receives

Ads are only personalised if you agree. The app never sends advertising your files, the titles you read, your library or your progress. No ad is shown while you read. Google processes its data under its own rules: policies.google.com/privacy.

Bifolio Pro

Buying Bifolio Pro goes through Google Play, which processes the payment under its own rules (policies.google.com/privacy). The app never sees your payment method; it only asks Google Play whether the purchase was made, and keeps that on the phone. With Pro, no advertising component is started anymore. As for any seller, Google Play gives us sales reports (order number, product, amount, country), without your name or payment method; we keep them for as long as our accounting obligations require.

Do not disturb

If you turn it on in the e-reader mode settings and allow it in Android settings, the app puts the phone in "Do not disturb" while you read and restores it afterwards. It does not read your notifications.

Google Play statistics

If you agreed to share usage and diagnostic data with Google on your phone, Google Play may show us anonymous, aggregated crash statistics. The app contains no audience measurement tool.

If you write to us

Your message and address are only used to reply to you.

Age

The app is intended for people aged 16 and over.

Legal basis

Providing the app's features you ask for (opening your files, keeping your library and progress, downloading a classic, reaching your server); your consent for personalised advertising and advertising trackers; the contract for the purchase; our legal obligations for sales reports.

How long

Everything the app keeps stays on your phone until you erase it or uninstall the app; the Android backup copy follows Google's rules for that backup. Your account data is kept as long as the account exists and erased when you delete it.

Erasing your data

In the app: remove a book from the library, a classic from the device or a server from the list; delete your account from Settings › your account › Delete my account, or without the app as explained at maxfabrics.com/ombelle/bifolio/en/supprimer-mon-compte. Uninstalling the app erases everything it keeps on the phone; your files stay in your folders. For your other rights, write to ombelle@maxfabrics.com. You can also complain to the CNIL (cnil.fr) or your local data protection authority.

If this policy changes

The date at the top of this page changes with it. Any new use of your data would first require your consent.
