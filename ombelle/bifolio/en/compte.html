Bifolio account terms

Version 1 · 25 November 2026

Bifolio works without an account. The account is optional and free: it keeps a copy of your library (titles, progress, settings, statistics, never your files) and brings it back on your other devices. Bifolio is published by Maxence Bonnet, sole proprietorship (MaxFabrics, SIREN 104406707), under the Ombelle brand.

Your account

You must be at least 16. You sign in with Google or with an email address of your own, which you confirm. Keep your access to yourself and tell us if someone else uses your account. The first name you choose is visible only to you.

Backup and sync

With an account, a copy of your library is kept online and your devices update each other. The files of your books do not travel: free books download again by themselves, and the progress of a personal file joins it when you add it on the other device. We do our best for sync to work without interruption or loss, but an outage remains possible: the app’s export (Settings › Backup) is still the best way to keep a copy of your own.

What is forbidden

Any illegal use, in particular: using someone else’s account, trying to read or change data that is not yours, bypassing the app’s limits, or using it to send emails in bulk.

Purchases

Bifolio Pro is bought once on Google Play, which bills and refunds it under its own rules; it applies on your devices signed in to the same Google Play account, with or without a Bifolio account. In case of dispute, write to us first; you can then refer the matter free of charge to the consumer mediator: Société Médiation Professionnelle, www.mediateur-consommation-smp.fr, Alteritae, 5 rue Salvaing, 12000 Rodez, France.

Suspension and end

You can delete your account at any time from the app (Settings › your account › Delete my account) or by writing to us: your library then stays on your phone, without an account. We may suspend an account that breaks these terms, proportionately, telling you why and how to contest. If we stopped the account service, we would tell you in the app at least 30 days before, and your library would stay on your phone.

Your data

What the account keeps, where and for how long: see the Bifolio privacy policy (maxfabrics.com/ombelle/bifolio/en/confidentialite).

Changes

If these terms change significantly, the app tells you and asks you to accept them before you keep using your account. You keep the guarantees the law gives you, including the legal guarantee of conformity. French law applies, without removing any right the law of your country gives you.

Write to us

Single point of contact, in French or English: ombelle@maxfabrics.com. A person answers you.
