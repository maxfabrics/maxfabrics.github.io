Tracking your reading: which app if you want to leave Goodreads?

Keeping track of your reading helps you read more and remember what you loved. Here's what really matters in an app, and how to leave Goodreads without losing anything.

What a reading log is for

Remembering what you read and what you thought, finding a quote again, seeing your habits (genres, pace, authors), setting a realistic yearly challenge, and knowing who borrowed that book that never came back.

What to check before choosing

  • Import: can you bring your whole history (ratings, dates, shelves) without retyping it?
  • Export: can you leave one day with your data?
  • Privacy: who sees your ratings and reviews?
  • Fast adding: barcode scan, title search, cover photo.
  • Social: public network or a circle of chosen friends.

Exporting your Goodreads library

On the Goodreads website, open "My Books", then "Import and export" in the left column, and click "Export Library". You get a CSV file with all your books, ratings, read dates and shelves. Keep it safe: that's the file other apps import.

The main alternatives

  • StoryGraph: strong on statistics and mood-based recommendations.
  • Babelio: large French-speaking community, reviews and lists.
  • BookHunter: library and reading log on your phone, with book lending between friends tracked on both sides.

Three habits that keep the log going

  • Write your take as soon as you finish a book, even two lines.
  • Save quotes as you read, not afterwards.
  • Pick a yearly challenge slightly above last year's, not double.

Doing it with BookHunter

BookHunter imports from Goodreads, Babelio, LibraryThing or a spreadsheet, and exports at any time. Add a book by scanning its barcode, searching its title or photographing its cover. Frequently asked questions

How do I import my Goodreads library into another app?

Export it from Goodreads (My Books, Import and export, Export Library), then import the CSV file into the app you chose. BookHunter accepts this file, as well as Babelio and LibraryThing exports.

Can I track books lent to friends?

Yes with BookHunter: request, loan, due date, reminder and return are tracked on both phones.

Do my friends see my ratings and reviews?

Not in BookHunter: friends see the shelf you choose to share, never your ratings or reviews.
